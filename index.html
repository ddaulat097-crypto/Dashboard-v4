Exhaustion Fade Mode (v3 experimental)
When ON: caps ratio at 0.85 globally. At ratio > 0.85, if near a zone (FVG/OB/swing/EMA within 30pts) AND RSI extreme (>70 or <30), FADES the verdict (flips direction). Continuation setups (ratio 0.70-0.85, supportive zone, opposite recent move) get auto-promoted to HIGH confidence.
Leave OFF for v4 baseline. Toggle ON only on v3.
M4 Trade Alerts (notifications)
When ON: phone notification when M4 signal fires (ratio ≥ 0.80, MODERATE+ confidence). Shows entry, stop, target prices.
Persistent — won't auto-dismiss until you swipe it. Lock screen visible.
Only fires while PWA is open or recently backgrounded. Will not fire when PWA is fully closed (Android may kill PWA after extended inactivity).
M4 Paper Trades on Chart (visual)
When ON: each M4 signal draws entry/stop/target lines on Chart tab (NAS100 5m). Lines play out live as price moves. SL/TP resolution is per-tick (every 2s).
Trade history button (top-right of Chart tab) shows all paper trades + totals.
Only NAS100 5m. Switching symbol/interval hides lines but keeps them tracked.
